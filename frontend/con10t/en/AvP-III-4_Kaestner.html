Introduction

The documentation in Volker Kästner’s volume on Pergamon was intended to record, both in drawings and, in particular, in photographs, all currently known fragments of the marble architecture of the Pergamon Altar, in order to make this material available for further research. However, including all of the photographic material available to date in the plate section of AvP III.4 would have made the volume excessively large. For this reason, some of the photographs have been transferred to this digital image catalogue.

These are mainly photographs of architectural elements of a uniform character, of which particularly large numbers have survived. The illustrations, identified in the book’s catalogue entries by the abbreviation “Suppl.”, comprise fragments of steps (building group O, Suppl. 1–14), base mouldings of the socle (building group N, Suppl. 15–35), crowning mouldings of the socle (building group I, Suppl. 36–42), cornices (building group H, Suppl. 43–65), column bases (building group F, Suppl. 66–106), column shafts (building group E, Suppl. 107–116), column architraves (building group C, Suppl. 117–140), geisa (building group B, Suppl. 141–200), standard cassettes (building groups A-V/M/H and NS, Suppl. 201–312), and gutter blocks (building group ZR, Suppl. 313–344).
