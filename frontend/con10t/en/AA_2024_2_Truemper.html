The supplement includes additional documentation for assessing and better understanding the Republican Baths at Pompeii: photos and plans of the Republican Baths and of Pompeian buildings cited as comparisons. The documentation is provided by members of the team and published here »su concessione del Ministero della Cultura – Museo Archeologicao Nazionale di Napoli« (Suppl. Fig. 1) and »su concessione del Ministero della Cultura – Parco Archeologico di Pompei« (Suppl. Figs. 2–55).
